# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Four friends use the app on their phones during an ordinary day. They check
their movement, compare the current ranking, log nutrition, share workouts,
and react to each other's progress. The primary context is quick mobile use
between activities, not long-form administration.

## Product Purpose

Enfermicambio turns automatic activity and nutrition data into a private,
playful competition for exactly four people. A successful experience makes the
next useful action obvious: connect health data, see today's position, log a
meal, share a workout, or check progress toward a mission. The app must remain
trustworthy about what is automatic, what is manually entered, and what is
private to the group.

## Brand Personality

Direct, energetic, competitive. The voice is friendly and occasionally
teasing, but never medical, shaming, or noisy. The visual direction should feel
like a focused sports club scoreboard: high contrast, fast to scan, and
rewarding without turning the product into a casino or a children's game.

## Anti-references

- Generic wellness dashboards that hide the main metric behind decorative
  cards.
- Clinical or medical interfaces that imply diagnosis, treatment, or health
  advice.
- Public social networks with discovery, follower mechanics, or engagement
  pressure.
- Casino-like gamification with random rewards, virtual currency, or excessive
  animation.
- Dense enterprise admin screens that make a four-person group feel like a
  large organization.

## Design Principles

- Make today's competition the first thing users can understand.
- Treat automatic health data and permission state as explicit product
  concepts, not silent background behavior.
- Use visual intensity to mark live competition and achievements, while keeping
  inactive content calm enough to scan.
- Keep every action recoverable: denied permission, offline data, and failed
  uploads must explain the next step.
- Celebrate progress without shaming users who have no data or are behind.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA practices where the mobile platform allows them. Do not use
color as the only indication of rank, freshness, permission, or failure. Keep
interactive targets at least 44 logical pixels, support text scaling, expose
meaningful labels to screen readers, and provide reduced-motion behavior for
state transitions. Permission and error messages must be understandable
without relying on technical platform terms alone.
